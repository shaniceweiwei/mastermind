How to play
  • Codes are 4 colors (repeats allowed). Before each game pick 6–10 colors — more colors, harder game.
  • right color, right spot · right color, wrong spot.
  • Versus: each player sets a secret code, then you take turns guessing each other's. Fewest guesses wins; the second player always gets an equal number of turns.
  • Co-op: the game makes a random code and you take turns cracking it together — 10 guesses for the team.
